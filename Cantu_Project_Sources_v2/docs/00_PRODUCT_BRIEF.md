# 00 — Product Brief

## Product

**Cantu** is a music-first language-learning web application. A learner brings a song they like; Cantu identifies the song, understands its lyrics, and turns the song into a progressive language lesson.

### Initial language pair

- Learning language: **Italian**
- Explanation/UI language: **Hungarian**

## Problem

People often hear foreign-language songs repeatedly without understanding them. Traditional language apps teach generic vocabulary detached from emotional context. Music gives repetition, rhythm, memory cues and motivation, but manually finding accurate lyrics, translating lines and deciding what is useful to learn is work.

## Promise

> Hallgasd. Ismerd fel. Értsd meg. Tanuld meg.

Cantu should let a Hungarian learner go from “I like this Italian song” to “I understand its idea and recognize useful phrases” in a short session.

## Core jobs to be done

1. **Identify my song** even when I do not own an MP3.
2. **Tell me what it means** without overwhelming me.
3. **Teach me memorable Italian from the song.**
4. **Let me go deeper** when I want to understand the complete lyrics.

## Product loop

`Hear song → identify → confirm → understand → learn → replay → remember → choose another song`

## Learning levels

### Level A — Song Snapshot

- title/artist;
- what the song is about in Hungarian;
- tone/theme;
- a handful of expressions to listen for.

### Level B — Quick Understand

- chorus first;
- 8–12 useful words/phrases;
- 3–5 important lines;
- compact grammar notes;
- short quiz.

### Level C — Deep Dive

- progressively more sections;
- line meaning and nuance;
- broader vocabulary;
- listening recall;
- eventually complete-song understanding when rights allow.

## Differentiation

Cantu is not primarily a lyrics viewer, karaoke app, translator or generic flashcard app. Its value is **pedagogical compression**: selecting the right parts of a song and turning them into a sequence that a learner can actually absorb.
