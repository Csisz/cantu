## Official legal sources

### Hungarian Copyright Act

1999. évi LXXVI. törvény a szerzői jogról

https://njt.hu/jogszabaly/1999-76-00-00

Relevant areas include exclusive rights, adaptation/translation, free-use interpretation, quotation and private-copying rules.

### EU InfoSoc Directive

Directive 2001/29/EC

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2001/29/oj

Relevant areas:

- Article 2 reproduction rights;
- Article 5 temporary-copy and other exceptions/limitations.

### EU DSM Directive

Directive (EU) 2019/790

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/790/oj

Relevant area:

- Article 4 text and data mining, including rights reservation.

### Digital Services Act

Regulation (EU) 2022/2065

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj

Relevant areas if the final service legally qualifies as hosting/intermediary:

- hosting liability conditions;
- notice-and-action mechanisms.

### GDPR

Regulation (EU) 2016/679

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj

Relevant principles:

- lawfulness/transparency;
- purpose limitation;
- data minimisation;
- storage limitation;
- integrity/confidentiality;
- accountability.

### Speech-to-text / language AI

Do not pin architecture to a single vendor in product docs. At implementation time:

- compare Italian transcription quality;
- verify EU/privacy terms;
- verify API retention/training/data-control terms;
- verify costs;
- use server-side credentials;
- keep adapters replaceable.
