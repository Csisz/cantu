# 07 — UI/UX Design System

## Design character

Cantu should be:

- musical;
- optimistic;
- clean;
- slightly illustrated;
- youthful enough for roughly teens/young adults without looking childish;
- calm during learning;
- expressive on the landing page.

## Existing visual assets

Keep and evolve the robot mascot. It creates a memorable bridge between AI and music.

Use robot motion with purpose:

- hero: personality;
- listening: attentive pose/waveform;
- success: celebration;
- lesson hint: small contextual appearance.

Do not place the mascot in every card.

## Landing vs app

### Landing

Can use:

- larger motion;
- scroll effects;
- video;
- oversized typography;
- narrative sections.

### Learning app

Should use:

- stable layout;
- less motion;
- short cards;
- persistent progress;
- generous whitespace;
- fast response.

## Updated hero CTA

Because v1 has one language pair, do not lead with “choose a language”.

Suggested hero:

**Tanulj olaszul a kedvenc dalaidból.**

Primary actions:

- `🎧 Hallgasd meg`
- `⬆️ Feltöltöm a dalt`

Supporting text:

> Nincs meg MP3-ban? Semmi gond. Játssz le néhány másodpercet, Cantu felismeri a számot.

## Recognition screen

A distinctive branded state can be one of Cantu's signature interactions.

Elements:

- pulsing waveform/rings;
- microphone state;
- timer/progress without fake precision;
- text: `Figyelek…`;
- cancel button;
- clear privacy microcopy.

## Candidate card

Large artwork, title, artist and two clear choices.

Primary: **Igen, ez az**

Secondary: **Nem ez**

Do not auto-advance on a high confidence score.

## Lesson cards

Recommended card types:

- `Miről szól?`
- `Figyeld ezt a refrénben`
- `Kulcsszó`
- `Mit jelent ez a sor?`
- `Nyelvi trükk`
- `Hallgasd meg újra`
- `Gyors teszt`

## Motion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Motion should signal:

- audio listening;
- progress;
- correct answer;
- transition between song sections.

Avoid continuous decorative animation inside reading exercises.
